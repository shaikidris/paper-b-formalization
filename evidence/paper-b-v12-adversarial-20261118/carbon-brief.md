The local source copy of TDT06 is the eight-page conference paper by Teslenko, Dubrova and Tenhunen, ISBN 972-8924-09-7, pp. 34–41, SHA-256 d8876d3440270ec2d67b037d65b68abf60a138a9ff3f92986b21df2914a22d09. The following are page-specific paraphrases, not the source itself.

- Section 2, pp. 34–35, uses a circuit DAG directed towards an output. Definition 1 requires the supplied vertices and putative cut to be disjoint. The cut must meet every supplied-vertex-to-output path. For each cut member there must be a path from some supplied vertex that meets that member and avoids the others.
- Sections 3–4, pp. 36–37, attribute a quadratic-time one-vertex construction and linear space for its chain to an earlier paper; per-chain side, index, and matching-interval information supports constant-time lookup. The 2008 thesis describes a later linear one-vertex method and quadratic total cost for its all-vertices chain construction.
- Section 5, pp. 37–38, reverses the topological ordering in the chain sub-vectors and fixes side ordering by unique vertex labels. Theorem 1 on p. 38 requires a member of a common double dominator to occur in the same position on the same side of both input chains.
- Section 6, pp. 38–40, lists the circuit, two vertices and the two vertices' individual chains as inputs. Figure 3 returns empty if either chain is empty and otherwise uses binary searches comparing the two sides. The result uses references to an input chain's two vectors and updated prefix lengths. Iterative combination is reported to cost O(k log n) for k supplied vertices. Section 7, pp. 40–41, separately times individual chains for reduced-tree vertices, and tests triples having the output as their immediate dominator. The paper does not state aggregate preprocessing time and retained size for its own method on all target vertices simultaneously.
- Choudhary's 2017 thesis, Theorem 3.2, states an O(mn)-preprocessing, O(n)-size, O(1)-query fixed-source oracle on directed graphs under two vertex failures.

Test whether these inputs and operations suffice, on their own or by a concrete reduction, for the four bounds claimed in the attached DAG manuscript. Compare the structural content of chain-prefix merging and the exact physical-tail recurrence. Separate what you can establish from the paraphrases and what would require the original pages. If the evidence supports a bounded novelty statement, suggest the exact wording. Report the decision you would advise before posting the revision and the smallest necessary changes.
