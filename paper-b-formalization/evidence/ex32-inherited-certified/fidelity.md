# EX3.2 inheritance and converse fidelity

The inherited contribution is gated by the endpoint's membership in the
child pair. The bypass proof preserves each physical tail while changing
the reachable continuation. In the converse, split by visitation of a
fresh endpoint. A witnessing path either gives an exact fresh endpoint
contribution or the inherited child contribution at a shared endpoint.
The proof quantifies over arbitrary nested cuts and includes disjoint cuts.
